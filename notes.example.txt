The mitochondria is the organelle responsible for producing ATP through cellular respiration.
Photosynthesis occurs in the chloroplasts of plant cells and converts sunlight into chemical energy.
DNA is composed of four nucleotide bases: adenine, thymine, cytosine, and guanine.
Adenine pairs with thymine, and cytosine pairs with guanine.
Enzymes are proteins that act as biological catalysts, speeding up chemical reactions without being consumed.
